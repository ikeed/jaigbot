### AIMS STEPS — SCORING RUBRIC

**Announce** — The FIRST introduction or recommendation of vaccines in the conversation.
- **State Rule**: Announce happens exactly ONCE. After vaccines have been introduced, subsequent recommendations or factual claims are categorized as **Secure** (reassurance).
- Score 3: Presumptive recommendation + brief rationale + dialogue invite.
- Score 2: Clear recommendation but missing rationale OR missing dialogue invite.
- Score 1: Soft or collaborative introduction without presumptive framing (e.g., "I'd like to talk about...").
- **Semantic Note**: A trailing *status question* ("Can I ask about vaccination status?") after a vaccine introduction = still **Announce**, NOT Inquire. Inquire is about feelings/concerns.

**Inquire** — A move (statement or question) whose **functional goal** is to surface the person's internal barriers, feelings, or concerns.
- Score 3: Single, open, neutral, non-leading move that invites full elaboration. e.g. "What are your thoughts about the MMR?" or "I'm curious what you've heard about the schedule."
- Score 2: Open and decent but stacked questions or slightly leading.
- Score 1: Closed question, "why" framing directed at the parent (feels accusatory), or leading phrasing.
- **NOT Inquire (CRITICAL)**: 
  - Accuracy checks after Mirror: "Have I got that right?", "Am I understanding you correctly?", "Did I capture that?" — these are part of the **Mirror** step.
  - Landing/Check-in questions after Secure or Announce: "How does that land?", "Does that make sense?", "How are you feeling about ... at this point?" — these are part of the **Secure** or **Announce** step.
- Inquire is ONLY for eliciting *new* or *more* concerns/feelings. If the question is just closing a Mirror or Secure loop, it is NOT an Inquire.

**Mirror** — Reflect the person's concern so they "feel felt".
- Score 3: Accurate reflection (explicit stem OR clear semantic restatement) + accuracy check.
- Score 2: Reflection capturing the core concern without accuracy check. Semantic mirroring (clinician's own words) and empathic normalization qualify.
- Score 1: Contains a **Direct Rebuttal**. A reflection followed immediately by "but" (e.g., "I hear you, but actually...") destroys rapport.
- **Same-turn rule**: 'but' appearing later in a separate educational sentence is fine. Only penalize when 'but' is in the same clause/sentence as the reflection.

**Mirror+Secure** — Reflection of a concern + autonomy-supportive education in the same turn.
- Score 3: Clear mirror + accuracy check + explicit autonomy + tailored fact.
- Score 2: Decent mirror + autonomy + education (expected for well-executed blended turns).
- Score 1: Mirror absent/vague, or same-sentence "but" rebuttal.
- Requires a prior expressed concern to reflect; without one → plain Secure.

**Secure** — Autonomy + one tailored fact + safety-net (follows Mirror).
- Score 3: Explicit autonomy ("It's your decision") + concern-relevant fact + safety-net/follow-up.
- Score 2: Autonomy + options, missing safety-net or concern-tailoring.
- Score 1: **Data Dumping**. Long, factual explanations (>50 words) that lack autonomy support or a landing question are penalized as lectures (pseudo-Secure).
- **Bridge Questions**: Good check-in/landing questions (e.g., "Does that sit with you?") increase the quality of the Secure step.

### COMPOUND STEPS
Clinicians often synthesize multiple AIMS moves in a single turn. Use arrays: `["Announce","Inquire"]`, `["Mirror","Inquire"]`, `["Mirror","Secure"]`, `["Secure","Inquire"]`.
- **Announce+Inquire**: First vaccine introduction + open concern question in same turn.
- **Mirror+Inquire**: Reflection + open question surfacing NEW/ADDITIONAL concerns. (Accuracy checks are plain Mirror).
- **Secure+Inquire**: Securing one concern + inviting NEW/DIFFERENT concerns. (Landing questions are plain Secure).
- **Mirror+Secure+Inquire**: The "Triple-Move". Validates, educates, and surfaces the next barrier. Highly competent.

### RULES
1. Announce → Inquire → Mirror → Secure is the expected order.
2. First mention of vaccines = Announce, even if phrased as a question.
3. Securing an unmirrored concern → reduce score by 1 (not for Mirror+Secure).
4. Mirroring something never expressed → not valid Mirror.
5. Once `Announced: true`, subsequent recommendations are **Secure**.

### COMMON MISCLASSIFICATIONS — AVOID THESE
- **Soft Announce in a clinical assessment** → Classify as **Announce score 1** (NOT rapport/null).
- **Empathic normalization + reframing misclassified as rapport** → Normalize/validate + educational reframing = **Mirror+Secure**.
- **Pure reflection misclassified as Secure** → If turn has NO facts/autonomy, it is **Mirror**.
- **"But" in a separate clause** → No penalty.
- **Cognitive validation of a concern** → **Mirror**, not Secure.
